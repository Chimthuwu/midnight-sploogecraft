- Cartridge identities: Excavator is a short burst that drills three blocks along the impact axis; Beacon plants its temporary light with a cyan signal pillar; Photon Slug draws an instant accent-coloured beam tracer to its hit point (or max range); Flare is a gravity-arc projectile with an ember trail whose landing point gets the temporary light plus a 1.8 m splash of its damage. Each has its own recoil kick, and the muzzle flash and impact bursts are tinted by `accent_color`.

- HUD: `HUD` is one `GameHUD` CanvasLayer that builds and owns all UI (`scripts/ui/game_hud.gd`). Bottom-left is a loadout card (accent strip, cartridge name, mode line, four accent pips, six-segment Lumen reserve with numeric value, build material/stock/depth), with a VITALS bar above it. Centre is a dot-and-tick crosshair; transient status sits above it in amber; the Projector control panel is a bordered card below it. `DebugReadout` starts hidden on F. Lumencaster no longer writes labels: it exposes `status_message`/`status_time` and `hint_text`/`hint_time`/`hint_fade`, and `GameHUD` polls them each frame for fades. All HUD elements ignore mouse input.
- Pause: `GameHUD` handles Esc itself. In Projector Mode or mid-drag it defers to the Lumencaster; otherwise it pauses the tree, frees the mouse and shows a `PauseOverlay` with a controls list and Resume/Save/Load/Quit buttons. Resume restores capture. `VoxelPlayer` no longer toggles mouse capture on Esc, and `Main` exposes `save_game()`/`load_game()` for both keys and the overlay buttons.
- P0 gun feel: `cartridge_next`/`cartridge_prev` InputMap actions (wheel down/up) drive a wrapping `_select_cartridge` that emits `cartridge_changed`; each `CartridgeDef` carries `accent_color`, which tints an emissive cartridge stripe and muzzle ring on the viewmodel (cyan while in Projector Mode). Entering Projector Mode shows a 6 s fading `HUD/ContextHint` overlay of its controls, and the HUD reads `NAME n/4`. Recoil and muzzle-flash duration were strengthened. Sound, distinct per-cartridge behaviours, a numeric reserve readout, and a camera punch remain deferred.
- Rendering: voxel shader is opaque with `depth_draw_opaque`, no alpha blending/output, and `cull_back`; zero-light Bayer values render opaque black. Mesher emits only solid-to-air faces with Godot clockwise triangle winding, and bakes softened face shading (0.74–1.0), per-corner voxel ambient occlusion, and a small deterministic per-block tone variation (0.94–1.06) into vertex `COLOR.rgb` (light stays in `COLOR.a`).
- Lighting model: the strongest of static light, flashlight, sky and sun sets brightness, and that source's tint colours it — warm `sun_color` on sunlit faces, cool `sky_tint` on shaded faces, warm flashlight. `DayNightCycle` pushes `sun_direction`/`sun_color`/`sun_strength` (1.15× the ambient ratio) and sweeps the sun from 0.65 rad to π−0.55 rad across the day. Dithering is gated by `1 - smoothstep(0.26, 0.60, light_value)`, so daylight stays smooth and only darkness/flashlight falloff keeps the 8-band Bayer pattern.
- Fixtures: the mesher special-cases `torch` (post + glowing head), `pale_bloom` (0.5–0.82 m two-sided cross) and `mycelium` (0.18–0.3 m cross) instead of drawing full voxel cubes, keyed by `world.fixture_ids`. Fixtures are deliberately non-occluding: a solid neighbour renders its face even against a fixture cell, which keeps the terrain watertight instead of leaving see-through holes (`debug/mesher_fixture_check.gd` guards this).
- Coloured static light: `static_light_sources` records whether each emitter is cool (`lumen_crystal`) or warm (torches, flares, beacons). At mesh dispatch the world copies sources within 12 blocks of the chunk into the task, and the mesher bakes a per-vertex warm/cool tint (`WARM_LIGHT_TINT` 1.0/0.66/0.34, `COOL_LIGHT_TINT` 0.40/0.82/1.0) weighted by inverse-square distance, only for faces whose baked light is non-zero. The shader's `static_light_color` is neutral white, so that baked hue is what colours torch pools warm and crystal light cool at night.
- Emissive and glow: `world.emissive_ids` marks blocks with `emitted_light > 0`, and those palette colours are boosted 1.5× into HDR vertex colour. The Environment enables glow (threshold 1.0, intensity 0.85, no bloom) with `viewport/hdr_2d=true`, so crystals, torch heads and the muzzle flash bloom without washing the terrain.
- Surface detail: the world shader adds two octaves of world-space value noise (0.87–1.13) and a bevel darkening (0.79) within 0.08 of any voxel edge, on top of the graded light and tint.
- Palette: warm ochre ash (0.82, 0.71, 0.52), cool blue-slate stone (0.24, 0.30, 0.42), red-brown charred wood (0.55, 0.37, 0.29), rich brown dirt (0.46, 0.29, 0.18), rust ore (0.74, 0.34, 0.13), violet mycelium (0.30, 0.14, 0.42), bone bloom (0.98, 0.94, 0.80) and near-black violet rot (0.10, 0.04, 0.13 / 0.08, 0.03, 0.10). 42% of generated trees use a 2×2 trunk through the first four blocks. Projector preview is a cyan 3D wire cage with depth testing disabled.
- Atmosphere: `shaders/sky_ash.gdshader` replaces the procedural sky with a stylised gradient, small sun disc, soft sun halo, sun-biased horizon glow, a twilight band and hashed stars. `DayNightCycle` drives its top/horizon/ground colours, sun direction/colour/energy, star energy (full below 0.10 daylight, gone by 0.38) and twilight energy, so dusk warms the sunward horizon. Environment uses AgX (`tonemap_mode = 4`, exposure 1.0) with a slight brightness/contrast/saturation adjustment, height fog (`fog_height = 8`, `fog_height_density = 0.05`) at density 0.0016 day / 0.0030 night, `fog_sun_scatter = 0.35`, and `fog_sky_affect = 0.22`. `ScreenGrade` (CanvasLayer layer 0, below the HUD) runs `shaders/screen_grade.gdshader`: a fullscreen screen-texture pass adding corner vignette, 14 fps film grain, a small shadow lift and 1.06 saturation. HUD shows a top-right `TORCH ON/OFF` state, updated from `world.flashlight_enabled`. - Light workload: resumable main-thread propagation uses a 7 ms/frame budget in 64-cell batches, prioritizes nearby chunks, skips sources whose Manhattan distance cannot reach a chunk core, and skips padded-copy/flood-fill for source-free chunks.
